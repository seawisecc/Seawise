## 1. Industry Management [IMS]

**Judul (EN)**

```
Industry Management [IMS]
```

**Industri (EN)**

```
Manufacturing
```

**Deskripsi (EN)**

```
Factories get stuck across scattered spreadsheets: stock counts never match, cost of goods is guesswork, and expiring materials slip past unnoticed. Seawise Enterprise brings purchasing, FEFO stock, formulas and INCI, production, and sales into one system, with real cost per batch calculated from the lot prices actually consumed.
```

**Tag fitur EN**

```
Purchasing & Approval, FEFO Stock, Expiry Control, Formula & INCI, Real Cost per Batch, Consignment & POS, Multi-user
```

**Studi kasus EN**

```
Challenge
Factories get stuck across a pile of separate spreadsheets. Physical stock does not match the records, cost of goods is only an estimate, and materials nearing expiry slip past unnoticed. Production decisions end up being made without accurate data, and margins erode quietly.

Solution
We brought the entire production flow into one system. Purchasing runs through a clear approval path, stock uses the FEFO method so older materials are consumed first, and product formulas are recorded properly along with their composition. Cost of goods is calculated automatically per batch from the lot prices actually used, so production costs show up exactly as they are.

Key features
Purchasing with an approval workflow
FEFO stock management and expiry control
Product formulas and material composition
Automatic cost of goods per batch
Consignment and POS
Multi-user with access control

Result
Reporting that used to be manual is now automatic, and cost of goods can be checked at any time without recalculating. The production and purchasing teams work from a single source of data.
```

# Catatan

- Kalau ada field EN yang kamu kosongkan, halaman `/en` otomatis memakai teks Indonesia. Jadi aman kalau mau dicicil.
- `Retail & Service [UMKM Edition]` gw ubah jadi `[SMB Edition]` di versi Inggris, karena istilah "UMKM" tidak dikenal pembaca asing. Kalau kamu mau tetap "UMKM Edition" sebagai nama produk, tinggal pakai judul aslinya.
- Semua teks bebas tanda em-dash sesuai preferensi kamu.
